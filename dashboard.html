Get Started

Use the AI Warehouse Assistant to record stock movements in plain English — e.g. "received 200 units of Dell Laptop in Mumbai Warehouse" or "dispatched 50 Dell Laptop from Mumbai Warehouse" — and the ledger updates automatically. Stock is tracked batch by batch (FIFO), so the oldest stock is always used first and anything sitting around for more than three months shows up here as an aging alert. You can also just ask things like "how much Dell Laptop do we have and where is it kept?" and the assistant will answer straight from the live ledger. The full workbook is always downloadable as inventory_report.xlsx above.
